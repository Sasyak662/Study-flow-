Delete your account

Deleting your StudyStarx account permanently removes your account and all study data synced to it (notes, flashcards, quizzes, plans, StudyQuest progress). This cannot be undone. Any Plus time left is lost.

Option 1: in the app

Open StudyStarx → Settings → Help, privacy & account → Delete my account.

Option 2: here

Signed up with Google? Delete your account in the app (type DELETE to confirm), or contact us at the support email shown in the app and we will delete it for you within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password)
  • All synced study data and progress
  • Your daily AI usage records

Copies in our safety backups are removed when those backups are replaced (within 30 days). Anonymous error reports and anonymous shared quizzes do not contain your details and are not affected.
