Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

StudyStarx is a study coach website and app. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We only collect what we need to run StudyStarx, and we never sell your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign up with a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, never the password itself. If you use Sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, your name and a Google account ID with us. We do not get your Google password, contacts, files or anything else.
  • Study information you add: your survey answers (such as name, grade, syllabus and subjects), notes, flashcards, quizzes, tasks, study plans, scores and StudyQuest progress. This is saved on your device and, when you are signed in, in your StudyStarx account so it syncs between your devices.
  • Questions to the AI Coach: the messages and files you send to the AI Coach and other AI features.
  • Classes: your display name, chat messages, questions and whiteboard drawings in a live class. Video and audio go directly between participants; we do not record or store them.
  • Purchases: if you buy StudyStarx Plus or a book, the payment is handled by our payment partner. We receive a confirmation of the payment, not your card or bank details.
  • Basic technical information: such as your IP address, which we use for a short time to stop spam and abuse (for example, too many login attempts).

2. How we use it

  • To run StudyStarx: your account, syncing, quizzes, flashcards, StudyQuest, classes and books.
  • To personalise your study material to your grade, syllabus and subjects.
  • To keep StudyStarx safe, limit misuse and enforce daily usage limits.
  • To process purchases and give you what you paid for.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Services that help us run StudyStarx

  • Google (Gemini AI): your AI Coach questions and the files you attach are sent to Google's Gemini service to create answers, quizzes and flashcards.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose it, to confirm your email address.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Upstash (Redis database): stores accounts and synced study data.
  • Cloudflare: may relay class video when a direct connection is blocked by a network. The relay passes the encrypted stream along and does not store it.
  • Payment partner (Razorpay): processes payments when that feature is switched on.

These services only get what they need to do their job.

4. Permissions on your device

  • Camera and microphone: only used when you join a live class and allow it.
  • Notifications: only used for study reminders you turn on.
  • Focus Lock (browser extension and Android app): during a focus session you start, it checks which website or app is open so it can block the ones you chose. It does not read your messages, passwords or screen content, and nothing about this is sent to us.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

Your study data stays in your account until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Security records such as login attempts are deleted automatically within days. You can clear the data stored on your device at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

6. Your choices and rights

You can see and change most of your information inside StudyStarx. You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account and data. You can stop using Sign in with Google at any time from your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party connections).

7. Children and students

StudyStarx is made for school and college students. If you are under 18, please use StudyStarx with the permission of a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians can contact us to see or delete their child's information.

8. Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, signed login sessions and rate limits to protect your account. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password and keep your recovery code safe.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you inside StudyStarx.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact the StudyStarx team at the support email shown when you sign in with Google, or through your account in the app.
